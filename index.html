Our story

Three Generations, One Recipe Book

Golden Crumb began as a single stone oven in a corner shop. Today, the same handwritten recipe book sits on our counter — dog-eared, flour-dusted, and still the final word on every loaf we bake.

We believe good bread can't be rushed. That's why our doughs rest overnight, our butter is folded by hand, and our bakers know every regular by name.

  • Family-run since 1998
  • Award-winning sourdough
  • Custom cakes for every occasion
